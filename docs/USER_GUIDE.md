# User Guide (Member & Manager)

Documentation HQ: [README](../README.md)

This guide is for non-admin users:

- `Member`
- `Manager`

**Goal:** Turn daily work into a sustainable rhythm so you can see real progress without losing the big picture.

---

## 1) The Big Picture: Why this works?

If you only "do work," you eventually get lost in details. This system breaks that cycle:

1. **Choose Focus** (Identify the most important thing).
2. **Execute** (Use the Timer & Work Log).
3. **Reflect** (Report + Ritual + Retro).
4. **Correct** (AI Sync + better decisions).

---

## 2) Roles: Member vs. Manager

### Member

- Focus on daily tasks.
- Use `Focus Task`, `Start`, and `Stop Session`.
- Log and review Work History.
- Use AI features for focus and correction.

### Manager (Team Lead)

Everything a Member has, plus:

- Team visibility (Lens: `My Team`).
- Review team Retros.
- Use Leadership Dashboards to identify bottlenecks.
- Lead team priorities based on data, not just "gut feeling."

---

## 3) Sustainable Rhythm (Daily/Weekly)

### Daily

1. **Start of Day** (5-10 mins): Choose a `Focus Task` or use `AI Suggested Next`. Click `Start`.
2. **During Day**: Stay focused on your task. Let the timer run to log a clean session.
3. **End of Day** (10 mins): Click `Stop Session`. Review your Work Log and Daily Report.

### Weekly (Member)

- **Mid-week**: Run `AI Progress Sync` to check for drift.
- **End of week**: Complete `Weekly Ritual` + `Retro` + `Weekly Report`.

### Weekly (Manager)

- **Start of week**: Align team priorities.
- **Mid-week**: Check for risks/delays via data.
- **End of week**: Review team Retros and make corrective decisions for next week.

---

## 4) Understanding the Atlas Interface

### Focus Task (Top Bar)

Your execution engine.

- `Choose Focus Task`: Manual selection.
- `Use Suggested`: Quick AI-based selection.
- `Start / Stop Session`: Session control.

### Focus Map

The visual hierarchy of your OKRs.

- Click items to navigate.
- Colors: `Needs care` (Red/Yellow), `On track` (Green), `Complete` (Blue).

### Inspector (Right Sidebar)

Details and editing for any selected item.

---

## 5) Harnessing AI: Your Smart Assistant

The AI features are designed to help you make better decisions:

- **Strategic KR Analysis**: Scores your "Efficiency" and "Effectiveness."
- **Suggested Next**: Picks the most critical task so you don't have to guess.
- **Weekly Summary**: Drafts your report narrative based on your actual work logs.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To get the best results from AI, read the **[AI Features Guide](AI_FEATURES_GUIDE.md)**. Quality of AI output depends directly on your input (clear titles and time estimates).

## 6) Common Troubleshooting

### 8. Start Button Not Working

- **Reason:** You must be the owner of the task to start the timer.
- **Note:** Starting a new timer automatically stops any existing active timer for you. You don't need to manually close other sessions.

### AI Suggested Next button missing

- AI needs enough context (Tasks, KR, progress) to make a valid suggestion.
- Ensure you have run `AI Progress Sync` recently.

### Changes not appearing

- Try "Reboot App" from `Manage App` or a "Hard Refresh" (`Ctrl+F5`).
